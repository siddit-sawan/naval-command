How to Play

Capture outposts for income. Destroy the enemy harbor to win.
Left-click / drag select ships  ·  Right-click move / attack
WASD / Arrows / middle-drag / screen edges pan (Shift faster)  ·  Wheel zoom to cursor
Ship cameras: select a ship, then V or the camera bar: Chase, Orbit, Waterline, Bridge  ·  Double-click a ship to ride along  ·  Esc overview
Q / E rotate  ·  R / F tilt  ·  Right-drag or Alt + drag orbit  ·  C face north
1–4 build ships  ·  Ctrl+A select all  ·  Space find selection  ·  H home
M mute  ·  Esc pause  ·  Submarines stay hidden unless enemies are close
Manual mode is a team deathmatch: 5 ships a side, sink them all. You steer and aim from the bridge.
WASD steer  ·  Mouse look  ·  Click / Space fire at the crosshair  ·  1–3 weapons
Shots land where you aim: lead moving ships using the range and flight time under the crosshair.
Missiles need a lock: hold the crosshair on a ship. Wheel / right-click binoculars  ·  Tab switch ship  ·  V chase view
Fog banks hide ships until they're close, and fog fronts roll in without warning.
Earn coins every battle and spend them on liveries in the store.
